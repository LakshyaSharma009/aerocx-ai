# Technical Fault Troubleshooting Guide (Synthetic)

## Recurring fault messages
1. Record the exact EICAS/ECAM message, flight phase, and engine serial.
2. Follow the Aircraft Maintenance Manual (AMM) fault-isolation task once; do not repeat it more than twice without engineering approval.
3. If a fault recurs 3 or more times in 7 days, open an engineering work order with fault codes, photos, and trend data.

## Vibration and oil-system indications
Treat vibration advisories plus high oil consumption as potentially safety-relevant: keep the aircraft on ground and call engineering immediately.

## Recommendations vs facts
- FACT: the steps above are the documented process in this guide.
- RECOMMENDATION: engineering may suggest borescope inspection based on trend data; that is an opinion, not a certified finding.
