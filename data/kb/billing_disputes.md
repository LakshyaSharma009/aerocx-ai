# Billing Disputes and Warranty Credits (Synthetic)

## Purpose
Resolve invoice queries for shop visits, power-by-the-hour (PBH) and parts.

## Procedure
1. Place the disputed invoice on hold so no dunning reminders are sent.
2. Reconcile each line against the contracted rate card and the approved work scope.
3. If a part was under warranty, issue a credit note within 2 business days.
4. Double-charged labour lines are corrected and re-invoiced with an explanation.

## SLAs
Acknowledge billing queries within 1 business day; resolve within 5 business days.
