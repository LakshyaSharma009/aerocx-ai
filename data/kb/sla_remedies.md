# Service-Level Agreement and Remedies (Synthetic)

## Response targets
- Critical (AOG / safety-adjacent): 1 hour response, 4 hour recovery plan.
- Major (maintenance delay, technical issue): 4 hour response, 24 hour plan.
- Standard (billing, scheduling, general): 1 business day response.

## Remedies
