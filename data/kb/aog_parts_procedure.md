# AOG Parts Expediting Procedure (Synthetic)

## Purpose
Restore aircraft-on-ground (AOG) situations caused by missing parts.

## Procedure
1. Confirm part number, serial effectivity, and approved alternatives in the illustrated parts catalogue.
2. Check pool stock at the station, then the regional pool, then the central warehouse — in that order.
3. If no pool stock exists, raise an AOG purchase order flagged URGENT-AOG; target supplier response is 4 hours.
4. Share the estimated time of arrival (ETA) with the customer every hour and offer an approved loan or exchange unit where available.

## Lead times
Standard replenishment is 48–72 hours; AOG expediting targets 4–12 hours depending on region.
