# Maintenance Delay Handling Policy (Synthetic)

## Purpose
Standard handling for departures delayed by unscheduled maintenance.

## Procedure
1. Maintenance control confirms the fault, the Minimum Equipment List (MEL) implication, and the estimated return-to-service time.
2. If the delay exceeds 2 hours, the customer support team issues a revised departure estimate and rebooking options for misconnected passengers.
3. If the delay exceeds 6 hours, a duty manager owns recovery: spare aircraft, re-accommodation, meals and hotel per local regulation.
4. The agent publishes customer messaging every 60 minutes until pushback.

## Escalation
Delays over 6 hours, or any delay affecting more than 100 passengers, must be escalated to the duty manager and logged as high severity.
